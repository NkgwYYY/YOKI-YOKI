## Performance

Geometry and fibre colors are cached at mount. The room uses 7,200 fibres; large portraits 14,000. Drawing is capped at 30 Hz, skipped for an unchanged pose, and paused when inactive, off screen or the document is hidden. No new image assets are downloaded. Measured on the review browser: large portrait rear frames 15–22 ms; the 390 px in-app colorful preview around 14 ms. These are browser measurements, not an iPhone hardware benchmark.
