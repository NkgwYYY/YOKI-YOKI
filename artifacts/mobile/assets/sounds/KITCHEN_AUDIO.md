# Egg atelier audio

`kitchen-simmer.mp3` (8 second loop) and `kitchen-lift.mp3` (1.15 seconds) are original procedural sounds created by `scripts/audio/compose_kitchen.py`, using seeded noise and synthesized bubble/metal tones. No sampled recordings or borrowed music are used.

Cooking starts sound inside the user gesture. Sound pauses on blur/background, stops with the pot, and releases audio resources on unmount. The independent sound button remains available beside the wallet. The existing village BGM preference is preserved.
