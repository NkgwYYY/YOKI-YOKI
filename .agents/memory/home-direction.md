---
name: YOKI YOKI home direction
description: Durable visual and interaction rules for the mobile app home screen.
---

Keep the home as one seamless, plain pink-lavender scene. Do not reintroduce a framed room, room title, item-description labels, patterned background, or a vertically stacked dashboard. Furniture and flowers appear only after purchase. The main actions stay visible around the character within one screen.

Use one shared currency name, 「きらめきポイント」, for food, furniture, flowers, and additional companions.

**Why:** A separate “atelier” card and pre-filled decorative room made the character feel pasted into a UI module rather than living naturally in the app’s world. Food-specific currency naming also stopped making sense once the wallet paid for decor and companions.

**How to apply:** When adding home features, preserve the unframed scene and fixed-height primary interaction area. Put secondary controls in sheets or menus rather than extending the home vertically. New purchasable decor must remain absent until owned.
