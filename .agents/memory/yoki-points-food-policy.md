---
name: YOKI points and food policy
description: Product terminology and the intentional split between retired shop food and the character feeding feature.
---

The shared balance used across rewards, feeding, eggs, and shop items is officially named 「YOKIポイント」 in all user-facing text. Do not rename or reset its existing storage keys or saved balances.

**Why:** The balance now pays for more than food, so older labels such as 「ごはんポイント」 and 「きらめきポイント」 are misleading and fragment one currency into several apparent currencies.

**How to apply:** New screens, errors, guides, rewards, and accessibility labels must use 「YOKIポイント」 whenever they refer to the shared balance.

Food products are permanently retired from the shop and item administration, but the existing hunger-feeding feature and its food choices remain part of the app.

**Why:** Shop food duplicated the character-care concept without adding durable customization value. Existing purchase history must remain intact rather than being deleted.

**How to apply:** Never seed, list, sell, or newly register shop items in the food category. Hide legacy food catalog records while preserving inventory history. Do not remove the feeding modal or its built-in food choices.
