---
name: Energy-charge world
description: Durable world-building rules for the character energy loop and its visual language.
---

The character itself is the energy source. Show the loop as character → warm flowing cable and visible plugs → transparent magical crystal tank → point exchange. Keep starry skies, glowing flower fields, distant fantasy scenery, and soft light particles.

Never reintroduce suns, sunshine, solar panels, solar generation, industrial power equipment, power-plant framing, or electricity-selling language. Active emitted energy and its connected glow must be fluorescent green; keep the surrounding sky, flowers, crystals, and YOKI-point accents pink-purple and fantastical rather than technical or industrial.

**Why:** The product direction explicitly replaced the former solar-power worldview, then standardized the character-to-tank energy path on fluorescent green so it reads as one connected flow. Mixing solar motifs or purple active-energy glows weakens that story.

**How to apply:** Use this rule for the energy tab, onboarding and guide copy, character lore, reward effects, future shop items, and any marketing that explains the daily-care reward loop. Preserve existing balances and rates when changing the presentation.
