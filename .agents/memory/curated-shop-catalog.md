---
name: Curated shop catalog
description: The durable merchandising and visual-quality rule for the YOKI YOKI shop.
---

Keep the shop intentionally curated: food, wearables, effects, nearby decor, backgrounds, and voice/sound should each present five polished choices rather than a large collection of generic items.

**Why:** The user explicitly preferred higher quality for every individual item over having many options. The shop should feel collectible and authored, not bulk-generated.

**How to apply:** When adding or replacing shop items, preserve the five-per-genre presentation unless the user changes this direction. Use cohesive, distinctive artwork with tactile softness and gentle cosmic details.
